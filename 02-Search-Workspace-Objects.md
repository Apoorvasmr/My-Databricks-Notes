# Chapter 2: Use Search to Find Workspace Objects

## Introduction

This chapter explains how to search for items throughout your Databricks workspace.

## Search Bar: Top of Home page

The home page search bar includes shortcuts for Notebooks, Tables, Jobs, Dashboards, and My assets, and can show items you recently used. You can search with a complete name, part of a name, or a natural-language description of what you need. Matching suggestions update as you type. Check the object type and owner to help identify the right result.

## Search Results Page

Press **Enter** after entering a search to see a larger results page. Refine the results using filters such as asset type, items owned by you, or **Certified** status. Result tiles provide details that can help you choose the right asset, including its owner, location, last-modified time, and relevant tags.

Selecting a result opens it in the current browser tab. To open it in another tab, use **Cmd + Click** on Mac or **Ctrl + Click** on Windows or Linux.

## Conclusion

In this lesson you learned how to:

- Search for workspace assets from the search bar at the top of the UI.
- Use asset details on the results page to compare possible matches.
- Apply filters to narrow results by asset type, ownership, and other characteristics.
- Search with natural-language descriptions or context instead of relying only on exact names.
