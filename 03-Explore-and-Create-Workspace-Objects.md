# Chapter 3: Explore and Create Workspace Objects

## Introduction

This chapter introduces the Workspace browser and explains how to create and manage items in your workspace.

## Workspace

Select **Workspace** in the left navigation sidebar to open the Workspace browser. **Home** is the starting location for your personal folders and items. The icon beside an item shows its type. Data assets such as tables, models, volumes, and stored functions are found in Catalog Explorer rather than in the Workspace folder.

Other areas in the browser include:

- **Shared with me** — Items other users have shared with you.
- **Workspace** — The workspace root folder, containing subfolders such as Repos, Shared, and Users. Access to other users' folders depends on your permissions.
- **Favorites** — Objects you have starred across Databricks.
- **Trash** — Deleted objects can be restored or permanently removed here. Items remain in Trash for up to 30 days before permanent deletion.

In the asset view, search within a folder or filter items by type, owner, or last-modified date.

## Create an Item

You can create a folder or object wherever you have write access. To do so, you can:

- Select **Create** and choose an object type.
- Right-click the destination folder, select **Create**, and choose an object type.
- Select **+ New** in the sidebar. This creates the item in your home folder, so you may need to move it to its final location.

## UI Features and Menus

The Workspace browser provides several tools for navigating and managing objects:

- Breadcrumbs at the top show your location.
- Separate menus provide actions for an object or its containing folder.
- Drag and drop lets you reorganize items; the **Move** dialog lets you choose a destination.
- You can share individual objects and folders.
- You can delete objects and manage their removal.

## Conclusion

In this lesson you learned how to:

- Navigate the Workspace browser.
- Create objects in your workspace folders.
- Move objects between folders.
- Share workspace objects with others.
- Delete objects temporarily or permanently.
