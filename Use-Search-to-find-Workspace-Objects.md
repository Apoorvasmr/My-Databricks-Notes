## Use Search to find Workspace Objects

### Introduction

This walkthrough shows you how to use Search to locate items throughout the workspace.

### Search Bar: Top of Home page

The search bar provides quick search buttons for Notebooks, Tables, Jobs, Dashboards, or My assets. It also displays assets you have recently interacted with. You do not need to know the exact name of an object as you can also use natural language to search based on context. You can use exact names, partial names, or context statements and the search dropdown will change dynamically to showcase potential items that match your query. Use the object type and owner to help narrow down your search.

### Search Results Page

When you hit **Enter** when typing in the search bar, you are taken to a Search Results page. This page provides a larger view of the potential results to your query. You can also filter your results based on additional criteria, such as an expanded list of asset types, assets specifically owned by you, or whether or not the item is classified as **Certified**. The tiles on the page include information about the asset to help you narrow down your search, including the owner, the location, when the asset was last modified, and additional tags and details that may be relevant in helping you find the right item. Selecting an item opens it in the same browser window, but you can use **Cmd + Click** (Mac) or **Ctrl + Click** (Windows/Linux) to open it in a new browser tab.

### Conclusion

In this lesson you learned how to:

- Search for assets with the search bar at the top of the Workspace UI.
- Use the search results page to refine your search by viewing additional asset information.
- Leverage filters on the search results page to narrow down your results based on different features and qualities of items in the platform.
- Enter a query using natural language or context terms to search using semantic information instead of exact asset names.
