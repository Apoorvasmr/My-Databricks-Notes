# Databricks Workspace UI

## Introduction

This walkthrough introduces the Databricks Workspace UI and its navigation options.

## Home page

Open the home page in either of these ways:

- Click **Databricks** in the upper-left corner of the UI.
- Click **Home** in the left navigation pane.

### Your resources

Your resources provides quick access to workspace assets:

- **Suggested** — Recent assets you have interacted with.
- **Favorites** — Assets and items you have starred across the platform.
- **Popular** — Assets receiving a high level of engagement from you or others in the workspace.
- **Mosaic AI** — AI models and features to try. Available in the full Databricks workspace only.
- **What’s New** — Recent features, releases, posts, and notifications. Available in the full Databricks workspace only.

Free Edition includes Suggested, Favorites, and Popular. A full Databricks workspace also includes Mosaic AI and What’s New.

## Navigation sidebar

The navigation sidebar is docked on the left side of the screen. Its top items provide common entry points to areas used across the platform.

To make more room in the workspace, click the icon in the upper-left corner to minimize the sidebar. When it is collapsed, hover over the left side of the screen to view the navigation. Click the icon again to restore it.

### New

The **+ New** button is a quick way to create workspace assets, including notebooks, queries, dashboards, Genie spaces, and jobs. You can also add or upload data from this menu.

### Compute

Compute in Free Edition is serverless and managed for you. The **Compute** menu provides access to SQL warehouses, Vector Search endpoints, and Apps. Free Edition has limitations on the compute objects you can create.

### Workspace

**Workspace** opens the workspace browser, where you can view, edit, share, and create Databricks objects such as notebooks, queries, and dashboards.

Typically, the user who creates an object owns it and is the only person or principal who can grant others access to it.

### Catalog

**Catalog** opens Catalog Explorer and Unity Catalog, where you can view data assets such as volumes, models, functions, and tables.

Catalog Explorer organizes items in a three-level namespace:

1. Catalog
2. Schema
3. Data object

This is the standard Databricks structure for referencing data objects. Catalog Explorer also provides access to permissions details, history, and lineage.

## Navigation sections

The sidebar groups tools into three core sections:

- **SQL**
- **Data Engineering**
- **AI/ML**

These sections help you find tools for your use case. Databricks Agents are not available in Databricks Free Edition.

## Key takeaways

After this lesson, you should be able to:

- Navigate the Lakehouse workspace home page.
- Identify the UI areas for Compute, Workspace, and Catalog.
- Access objects you own and data assets in Unity Catalog.
